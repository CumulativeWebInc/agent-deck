What it does

Feed it your own output about the CWI catalog and get back a scored, annotated playback — every detected claim checked against the verified Scoreboard Chip fact set, returned with a verdict, a citation with an observed date, a fidelity score 0-100, and a band. UNVERIFIABLE is a verdict, never a pass.

Note: Prototype complete 2026-09-16 (spec, working checker CLI, 6/6 test gates green). Not yet deployed.
